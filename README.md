DayFlow – Human Resource Management System
Proposed Solution

We propose DayFlow, a centralized Human Resource Management System designed to simplify and automate essential HR operations through a single platform.

Our system provides separate role-based access for Employees and HR Administrators. Employees can manage their profiles, track attendance, submit leave requests, and view salary information, while HR administrators can manage employee records, monitor attendance, approve or reject leave requests, manage payroll details, and access HR reports.

To make our solution more effective than a conventional HRMS, we introduce an "Employee Wellness & Engagement Monitor". Using existing attendance and leave data, DayFlow identifies unusual workforce patterns such as frequent absences, repeated leave patterns, and declining attendance trends. Instead of diagnosing employees, the system provides transparent indicators and alerts that help HR identify situations that may require attention.

We also provide "Smart HR Alerts" that highlight important workforce situations, such as attendance decline and leave concentration, allowing HR administrators to respond proactively.

Through centralized data management, automation, role-based access, workforce insights, and reporting, DayFlow reduces manual HR effort, improves transparency, and supports better workforce management.



 Process Flow

 Step 1 – User Authentication

Employees and HR administrators securely register and log in using their credentials. Role-based access ensures that each user can access only the features permitted for their role.

 Step 2 – Employee Management

Employees can view and update permitted profile information, while HR administrators can manage employee records, job details, documents, and other employee information.

 Step 3 – Attendance Tracking

Employees perform daily check-in and check-out actions. DayFlow records attendance and maintains daily and weekly attendance information with statuses such as Present, Absent, Half-day, and Leave.

### Step 4 – Leave Management

Employees submit leave requests by selecting the leave type, date range, and remarks. HR administrators review the requests and approve or reject them. The employee's records are updated accordingly.
 Step 5 – Payroll Visibility

Employees can securely view their salary and payroll information, while HR administrators can manage salary structures and payroll-related details.

Step 6 – Employee Wellness & Engagement Monitoring

DayFlow analyzes attendance and leave patterns to identify unusual changes such as frequent absences, repeated leave patterns, or declining attendance trends.

The system generates a **Wellness Indicator** based on these work-related patterns and provides transparent insights for HR review.

### Step 7 – Smart HR Alerts

The system automatically highlights important workforce patterns such as:

* Attendance decline
* Repeated absences
* Unusual leave patterns
* High leave concentration
* Reduced team availability

These alerts help HR administrators identify situations that may require attention.
 Step 8 – Analytics & Reporting

HR administrators can view dashboards and reports related to employees, attendance, leave, payroll, and workforce trends. This helps HR understand workforce activity and make informed decisions.

Step 9 – Notifications

The system provides notifications for important HR activities such as leave requests, approval or rejection updates, and other relevant alerts.


 Key Innovations

Employee Wellness Indicator

Uses existing attendance and leave patterns to highlight possible workforce concerns without collecting sensitive personal information or making medical/mental-health diagnoses.
 Smart HR Alerts

Automatically identifies important workforce patterns and brings them to the HR administrator's attention instead of requiring HR to manually search through employee records.
